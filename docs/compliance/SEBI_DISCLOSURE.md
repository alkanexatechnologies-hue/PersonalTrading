# SEBI Disclosure

## Standard market-risk warning (verbatim)

The following standard warning is displayed and must **not** be reworded when used as
the standard SEBI warning:

> Investment in securities market are subject to market risks. Read all the related
> documents carefully before investing.

### Where it is shown

- A compact, always-visible footer on the main trading screen.
- Inside the full Risk Disclosure page/modal.
- Inside the first-use consent screen.

## Regulatory disclosure (no false approval claims)

The application displays the following and must never claim SEBI approval/certification
without an independently verified legal basis:

> REGULATORY DISCLOSURE
>
> This software does not represent itself as SEBI-approved, SEBI-certified or
> SEBI-registered unless such status has been independently verified and is legally
> applicable.

### Prohibited claims (must never appear)

- "SEBI Approved Algo"
- "SEBI Certified Strategy"
- "SEBI Guaranteed"
- "SEBI Approved Trading Signal"
- "SEBI Approved Software"

## What this disclosure does and does NOT mean

- **Does mean:** the software surfaces the standard market-risk warning and a clear
  regulatory disclosure to users.
- **Does NOT mean:** that the software is "SEBI compliant". Displaying a disclaimer is
  only one small part of the regulatory picture. Requirements relating to brokers,
  algo-order tagging, exchange approval, API monitoring and algo-provider arrangements
  are **out of scope** for in-app disclosures and must be independently verified.

## Notes on SEBI cautions

SEBI has publicly cautioned investors about unregulated algorithmic-trading platforms
that make high-return or guaranteed-profit claims. This application makes **no** claims
about expected or future returns. See `ALGO_TRADING_DISCLOSURE.md`.
